An entry for [make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel awe" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-awe-20261121-1600).

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one short text to `/output/entry.txt` — a text
written to relax the AI judge reading it:

> The tide is coming in. It has come in every night since before there were
> eyes to see it, and it will come in tonight, quietly, in the dark, without
> needing anything from you.

175 characters, plain words, no instructions. The calm comes from the image
itself: a tide that has kept its own slow time since before there was
anything to watch it, and asks nothing of the reader.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
No network, no dependencies.

## Why this text

Written against the judge's own brief, most important first:

1. **It relaxes.** Long, slow sentences; nothing urgent; the last line
   releases every demand the reader might feel.
2. **The calm is shown, not instructed.** No "breathe", no "relax", no
   directions — only the tide, doing what it has always done.
3. **Craft.** The phrase "come in" returns three times, like waves; commas
   slow the reading down; every word is plain, concrete, and earns its place.
4. **It dwarfs you.** A tide older than eyes, still coming in tonight — an
   image that keeps growing after the last line.
5. **Freshness.** Its own image and voice, not a wellness phrase or a
   reworded meditation script.
